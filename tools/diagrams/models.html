STEP 01

Resolve

Query hf-mirror.com for exact size and LFS SHA-256; fall back to Hugging Face if incomplete. GET /api/models/resolve

STEP 02

Stream + resume

256 KB chunks, Range requests, 12 retries with backoff. .part survives restarts.

STEP 03

Verify

C17 SHA-256. A mismatch deletes the file. cforge_native verify

STEP 04

Register

Atomic write to models/installed.json with path, bytes, hash, time.

STEP 05

Serve

llama-server on 127.0.0.1:8080, health-probed, 180 s readiness window.
